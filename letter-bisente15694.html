My once dearest friend,

Thank you for being the story I once chose to step into. As the new year comes, I’ll let you remember me however you wish—if you believe I ever asked too much of you, so be it; if you think I lingered in your presence and felt your absence too deeply, then let that be your truth.

I’ve learned that no amount of explanation can reach someone unwilling to see beyond their own perspective. And yet, despite everything—despite my detachment, and despite the hope I carried for what remained of our time—I want you to know that I forgive you.

Happy New Year, Bisente!

— Kai
